Our story

Travel should feel personal.

WanderLux is a fictional travel agency created to demonstrate an accessible, responsive travel-planning website for ICT502.

Our mission

We believe good journeys begin with clear information. Our aim is to make it simpler for travellers to explore options, plan around their budget and feel confident asking for assistance.

That means avoiding confusing processes and giving visitors the information they need before taking the next step.

Our values

Clear travel estimates and easy-to-read information.

Care

Choice

Flexible destinations, duration and travel style.
